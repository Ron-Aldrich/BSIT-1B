Where the words come from

Ethics

Comes from the Greek word ethos, which means “custom.”

Morality

Comes from the Latin word mos or moris, which also means “custom.”

What is ethics?

As a subject of study: ethics is about determining the grounds for the values with particular and special significance to human life.
As a science: ethics is a normative philosophical science, a theoretical science of good and bad, or right and wrong.
What it provides: the principles of the morality of human acts.

In general

Ethics is about the good things we should pursue and the bad things we should avoid, and about the right ways in which we could or should act versus the wrong ways of acting.

Difference between Ethics and Morality

EthicsMorality
ApproachReasoning involvesAdhere to what is described
RulesBeyond rulesHard and fast rules
ScopeUsually considered universalRelative to society/culture
SurvivalFor the survival of the societyFor the survival of the individual

Memory hook: ethics asks why and reasons beyond the rules; morality follows the rules a society or person already holds.

Class dilemmas

Sino ang pipiliin ko?

Mahal ko o mahal ako?

Who will you save?

Your wife was diagnosed with a severe cancer, and the only thing that can save her is a surgery. Doing this will end your unborn child’s life. Your wife is pleading with you to save your child. What will you do? Who will you save: your wife or the child in her womb?

There is no single answer to memorize. Ethics is about the reasoning behind your choice, so practice explaining the values you weighed and why.
